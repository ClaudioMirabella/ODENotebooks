How to type mathematics

When you see red

A red box is an error message, not a disaster. Read its last line: it says what went wrong. In the notebooks of this course a line just above or below the red box, starting with Hint (MATH 331), often says what to do.

Still stuck? Copy the last line of the message and bring it to class or to office hours.

Sliders

A plot with a slider works only after its cell has run: until then the notebook shows a fixed picture and a line that says so. Before you move a slider, predict what will happen; then check. You can also click a slider and use the arrow keys.

Change things

You cannot break anything: the notebooks run only inside this browser tab. Change a number, run the cell again, see what happens. If a red AssertionError appears, you changed a number that the notebook compares with a value of the book: put it back, or delete that line.

Keep your work

Your changes are saved only in this browser, on this computer. They are lost if you clear the browser data, use a private window, or change computer. To keep a notebook, or to hand it in: File > Download.

Start again from the original, or get a corrected version

Once you have run or changed a notebook, the browser keeps showing your version, also when your instructor has corrected the notebook on this site. To get the notebook of the site: open File > Open... (the list of files), right-click the notebook, choose Delete, then come back to this page and open the notebook again.

What this site knows about you: nothing

There is no login, and there are no cookies, trackers or advertisements. Python and the notebooks are downloaded from this site only; your code and your answers never leave your computer.

If it does not work on your device

Use a recent version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a laptop; phones and old tablets may be too slow. Every plot with sliders also has a fixed version in the notebook, and the course videos and handouts need no Python. Tell your instructor: there are computers you can use.
